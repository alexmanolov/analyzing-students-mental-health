## Data Description

The dataset used in this project comes from a 2018 survey conducted at a Japanese international university. Due to licensing and ethical constraints, the full dataset is preloaded in DataCamp and not publicly distributed.

**If you wish to reproduce this project,** you may use a mock dataset or contact the university's research department for access.

Key fields include:
- `student_id`: Unique identifier
- `is_international`: Whether the student is international (1) or domestic (0)
- `depression_score`, `stress_score`, `social_support_score`: Numeric scores from validated questionnaires
- `length_of_stay`: Time spent in host country (months)
